# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Mes Que Du Foot Publisher ("the App") is a web tool that lets football content creators connect their TikTok account and publish their own videos to TikTok. This policy explains what data the App uses.

## Data we collect

When you log in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and the basic profile information that you authorize (display name, avatar, and the creator information TikTok provides for posting). The App also processes the videos and captions that you choose to publish.

## How we use data

Data is used only to publish the videos you select to your own TikTok account, with the settings you choose (caption, privacy level, interaction settings). We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as needed to publish your content. Videos are deleted from our systems once they have been sent to TikTok. You can revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions).

## Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete your data at any time by writing to mesquedufoot@gmail.com.
